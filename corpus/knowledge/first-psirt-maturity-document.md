## Package rules

1. **Level-attribution rule:** when a package invokes this document, cite it as FIRST's *maturity* document and name the level explicitly (Basic / Intermediate / Advanced). Do not convert a level into a score, a certification or a mandate (C2, C4).
2. **Foundations-first rule:** a maturity statement that omits the operational-foundations floor (sponsorship, stakeholders, budget, policies) is not what this document says (C6, C7). "To some degree" is the document's own hedge.
3. **Precondition rule:** Level 2 is gated on Level 1 in the document's own words (L288). Three independent parallel tracks are unsupported.
4. **Reachability rule:** Level 1 intake is a published, reachable contact point; the concrete artifact named is a PSIRT email address with a PGP key (L250, L406). "Recommended" is the strength for the PGP key.
5. **Yardstick rule:** a team must pick a scoring yardstick and, if not CVSS, explain the choice to customers (L270, L271). A score is not a qualification — the qualification gate stays with F44/S098.
6. **Bypass-scope rule:** the established-finder fast path at Level 3 bypasses *triage* and nothing else (L339).
7. **Template rule:** the triage interval in the policy template is the document's unfilled `[#]` placeholder (L393). Any package quoting a triage SLA to FIRST is misreading a template slot.
8. **No-certification rule:** this document certifies nothing. Its own notice is informational (S101 L8), the term is a profile (L224), and the content body carries zero occurrences of "shall" and zero of "certif" (measured over S100 L216-L464).
