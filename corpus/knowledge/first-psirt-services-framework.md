## Falsifiable package rules

1. **Catalogue-attribution rule:** when a package invokes this page, cite it as FIRST's *possible services* catalogue (L219) with the version 1.1 title (L1). Do not convert a service, function or sub-function into a requirement, and do not quote a cardinality without recounting the headings (C2).
2. **Qualification-gate rule:** a finding's qualification is decided by the receiving team's documented quality gate or bug bar (L853, L857), with CVSS severity as an input. A package claiming "qualified" from a score alone, or assuming the finder's severity label is the gate, is unsupported; establish the target's bar first.
3. **Reproduction rule:** validation requires a reproduction in an isolated environment (L905, L918). A report without reproducible conditions is at most unvalidated, and a reproduction performed on production is outside the stated method.
4. **Established-finder rule:** a reputation-based bypass is a recommendation covering qualification and triage (L875), conditional on the team's judgement; it never establishes a finder's right to skip intake, reproduction or disclosure.

## Rejected readings

- **"The framework makes PSIRT services mandatory / defines a compliance standard." — rejected.** The page calls the frameworks high-level documents of services a team *may* provide (L219) and says they exist to assist organizations building capabilities (L219-221); no normative "shall" vocabulary or certification is claimed in the capture, and version 1.1 is a document version, not a standard designation.
- **"Established finders skip the whole process." — rejected.** L875 names qualification and triage as the bypassed functions and routes the report into root cause analysis and remediation development. Intake, reproduction and disclosure remain.
- **"CVSS severity is the qualification decision." — rejected as an attribution.** L853 presents CVSS as producing a score that can be translated into a qualitative label, and the very same paragraph introduces quality gates and bug bars as the documented criteria (L853-857). F01/S001/S002 already record severity-versus-risk separation.
