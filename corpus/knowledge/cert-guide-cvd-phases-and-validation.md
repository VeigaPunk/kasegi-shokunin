## Falsifiable package rules

1. **Phase label and model rule:** when a package invokes this page, retain CERT Guide attribution and its adaptation of ISO/IEC 30111:2019. Do not infer universal phase cardinality from the six summary headings (L60-70); the role table separates Validation and Prioritization (L129-140), and L73 acknowledges differing models.
2. **Role-column rule:** recover every table assignment using L112-116 plus the relevant complete row. For Validation (L129-134), Vendor/Coordinator receive the validation cells; Reporter does not. For Remediation (L141-146), the Reporter cell is fix confirmation, not report validation.
3. **Response/deployment rule:** classify evidence by the action and actor the source actually names. Prioritizing a report for response (L138-139), confirming a fix (L143), public disclosure (L69), and deploying a fix or mitigation (L158) are different evidence items. An advisory alone does not establish deployment under this model.

## Rejected readings

- **“CVD has exactly six universal phases.” — rejected.** The six summary headings are one presentation of CERT/CC's adapted model (L57-70), not a mandate. The source explicitly acknowledges models with varying phases (L73); even this page's role table separates Validation and Prioritization (L129-140). A universal mandate would require separate authority not supplied by S095.
- **“The reporter must validate incoming reports.” — rejected as an attribution to this page.** In the Validation row, Reporter is a dash (L131), whereas Vendor/Coordinator have validation cells (L132-133). Reporter fix confirmation belongs to Remediation (L141-143). A dash is not a prohibition on reporters checking findings, and the page does not remove a researcher's responsibility to support their report.
