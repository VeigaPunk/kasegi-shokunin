[note] The live root carries every seat's scratch, and the round-41
container kasegi-r41 is itself a family match, so a live-root scan is
expected to red on the container whenever the chain to --work is not
clean (F2).  Any read=fail above is also a real scan error: a single
unreadable directory anywhere under the root (e.g. in another lane's
scratch) fail-closes the whole scan by design.  The suite must scan an
isolated root, or place --work on a clean chain inside the container,
exactly as the round-40 wave-1 reference limb documented.
