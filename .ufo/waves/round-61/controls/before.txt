1:# CWE Top 25 2025 Methodology
2:source: S013 — https://cwe.mitre.org/top25/archive/2025/2025_methodology.html — “Removal of CWE Mapping Normalization”
7:When a report cites the 2025 CWE Top 25, identify it as an aggregate ranking for that edition and map the reported root cause to a CWE using the technical evidence, not rank alone.
9:Any year-over-year comparison must disclose the change from View-1003 normalization to the 2025 use of provided CWE mappings, and state that the ranking score used CVSS 3.0 or 3.1 records.
10:A reviewer can compare the report’s CWE, edition, and trend statement with the cited methodology; if the technical root cause or comparison basis is missing, mark the inference unsupported.
13:- support the report’s root-cause CWE with case-specific technical evidence;
14:- state the mapping-normalization change when comparing with earlier years;
17:- do not select a case’s CWE solely because it appears in the ranked list.
23:- S013, “Removal of CWE Mapping Normalization”: “For the first time in 2025, the Top 25 used the actual CWE mappings as provided, without normalizing them back to View-1003.”
25:- S014, “Abstraction”: “For root cause mapping, CWE recommends that Base and Variant level CWEs should be used whenever possible”. This is MITRE’s mapping guidance.
26:- S014, “Possible Causes of Rank Shifts”: “The shift in scoring and the fewer CWE mappings in NVD data in 2024 also contributed to this.” MITRE lists methodology and data changes as possible contributors, not a single proven cause.
32:A CWE mapping can make a root-cause explanation more precise, but it does not replace reproduction, affected-asset scope, or a program-specific impact assessment.
41:S015 supplies the ranked 2025 list but does not replace its methodology or a technically supported root-cause mapping.
