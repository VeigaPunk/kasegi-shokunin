- **Context (not a contradiction, but the reason it is only low severity):** the cna entry references S030 twice in its **body** — line 16 "S030 pins 4.2.0 (effective 2026-08-25) and must not silently become a claim about 4.2.6" and line 69 "S030 is kept as the pinned earlier version" — and S030 is the superseded predecessor of S031 (same row family, registered round 23). So the row is content-attached but not header-attached. Under a strict header-based citation-coverage scan (the R9 rule as framed), S030 has no citing entry; the used-by cell names an entry whose header does not cite it, so the used-by edge does not close the gap.
- **Suggested disposition (for the L1/scribe, not this seat):** either record S030's superseded status so the census/coverage checker can exempt it, or have the cna entry's header carry both S031 and S030 (Q1 allows multiple `source:` lines — precedent: `statutory-authorization-and-unauthorized-access.md` headers S033/S034/S035).
