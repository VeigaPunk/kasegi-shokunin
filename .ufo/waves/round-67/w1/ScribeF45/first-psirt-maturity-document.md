## Falsifiable package rules

1. **Level-attribution rule:** when a package invokes this document, cite it as FIRST's *maturity* document and name the level explicitly (Basic / Intermediate / Advanced). Do not convert a level into a score, a certification or a mandate (C2, C4); the document defines maturity as a profile (L224) and describes use cases a team might select (L221).
2. **Foundations-first rule:** a maturity statement that omits the operational-foundations floor (sponsorship, stakeholders, budget, policies) is not what this document says (C6, C7). "To some degree" is the document's own hedge (L292) — do not upgrade it into a completeness test, and do not downgrade it into optionality either.
3. **Precondition rule:** Level 2 is gated on Level 1 in this document's own words (L288). A package that presents the three levels as independent, parallel tracks is unsupported.
4. **Reachability rule:** Level 1 intake is a published, reachable contact point, and the concrete artifact named on the checklist is a PSIRT email address with a PGP key (L250, L406). A package that describes a Level-1 PSIRT keeping intake private contradicts C8. Note "recommended" for the PGP key.
5. **Yardstick rule:** a team must pick a scoring yardstick and, if not CVSS, explain the choice to customers (L270, L271). "Ideally" is the strength of the CVSS recommendation; the explanation duty is stated plainly. A score is not a qualification — the qualification gate stays with F44/S098 (C10).
6. **Bypass-scope rule:** the established-finder fast path at Level 3 bypasses *triage* and nothing else (L339). It never establishes a finder's right to skip intake, validation or disclosure (C14).
7. **Template rule:** the triage interval in the policy template is the document's unfilled `[#]` placeholder (L393). Any package quoting a triage SLA to FIRST is misreading a template slot (C16).
8. **No-certification rule:** this document certifies nothing. Its own notice is informational (S101 L8), the term is a profile (L224), and the capture carries zero occurrences of "shall", "certif", "conform" or "compliance" in the content body (measured over S100 L216-L464: `grep -c` = 0 for each; `grep -c '\bMUST\b'` = 1, at L292, aimed at the team and not at a reader).
