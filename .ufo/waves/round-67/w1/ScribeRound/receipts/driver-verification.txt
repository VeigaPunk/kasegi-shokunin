## The `COLLECTOR ignored=… reason=AttributeError` line is benign — and must not be read as a silent skip
The multi-head battery's `--collector` argument is OPTIONAL and its docstring says the collector is
"a Python file defining `probe_table(root)`". The committed probes collector
(ExecMultiHeadProbes/probes.py) does NOT define `probe_table` (it is a CLI harness), so the
battery's `load_collector` catches the AttributeError and falls back to its OWN built-in
`probe_table(root)` — 14 named probes (P1..P14) with their expected ok/stale+reason, written
independently of the probes collector. The fallback is printed, not silent, and the battery's own
`MUTBAT killed=7/7 survivors=0 equivalents=3 invalid_rejected=0 failures=0` line is the PASS
criterion the driver compares against the pin. The driver additionally records the collector line in
the RESULT note, so a reader sees it.
**However**: the battery therefore runs on its BUILT-IN table, not on the committed probes
collector, so the two probe sets are not the same set. That is a design fact, not a defect, and it
does not weaken the 7/7 kill result — but the L1 should not describe the multi-head battery as
"driven by the committed probes collector", because it is not.

## Limits
This is a TRIAL run over a fixture commit of the untracked wave tree, in `--candidate` (unpinned)
mode, not a `--final` run over a committed lane head. It carries **no** claim: it certifies that
the current driver bytes green the honest candidate, nothing about a freeze, a measured revision,
a content pin or a landed implementation. The L1's final committed-input measurement is still owed.
