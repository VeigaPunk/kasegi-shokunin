mirror-after-cd is a measurement variant of mirror-after in which every knowledge entry's
`recheck:` line has its `cd <lane>` prefix retargeted onto the mirror root (recheck lines only;
entry prose untouched). Reason: recheck-audit's (b) cwd / (d) cdroot limbs and the frontier
harness execute the line with `cwd=<root>` and the line's own `cd` overrides it, so the
landed-byte form can only be exercised against the real lane. The variant is a shape check;
the landed-byte form's lane-root result must be re-measured after the L1 lands the same bytes.

## Note 1 — registry count is 59, not the as-frozen 58

Base registry is 55 rows (S001–S055; `grep` above on mirror-base). The claim appends four rows —
S057 (mined) plus S056/S058/S059 (rejected) — so the after-count is 55+4 = 59. Measured 59 on the
composed after-mirror. This matches `.ufo/waves/round-39/claims-erratum-1.md` E1 ("55 → 59"); the
as-frozen 58 counted only the three rejected rows.

## Note 2 — lane-root-bound instruments

`recheck-audit.py` (cdroot limb: `<path>` in `cd <path> &&` must equal the audited root) and
`frontier-recheck-count.py` (executes the entry's own line with cwd=<root>; the line's
`cd <lane> &&` then binds execution to the real lane) cannot pass on a mirror whose entries keep
the landed lane prefix. Both were run on the real lane tree as well (transcript F). After the L1
lands the same bytes, the authoritative after-run is at the lane root:
`python3 corpus/instruments/recheck-audit.py --quiet` and
`python3 corpus/instruments/frontier-recheck-count.py`, which the mirror predicts to be
`expected=51 ... exit=0` and `entries=25 pass=25 exit=0`.
