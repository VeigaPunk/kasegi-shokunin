FINDINGS
--------
F1  Fence-closer length is stated two ways and they disagree on a legal document.
    · The suite-build item text: "closer is exactly three backticks or three tildes
      followed by optional spaces, same type as opener".
    · The G1 fixture model (ExecWholeTailFixture README + g1-check.sh, CommonMark):
      "a line of the SAME character with a run at least as long as the opener, followed
      by blanks, closes it"; a backtick fence's info string may not contain a backtick.
    Evidence: probe U1 (4-backtick opener, 3-backtick mid, 4-backtick closer) is CLOSED
    under the fixture model and OPEN under a literal "exactly three" implementation,
    because the 3-backtick line would close the 4-backtick fence early and the trailing
    4-backtick line would then open a new one. Measured with a literal exactly-three
    closer on the same probe file:
        U1 under literal-exactly-three closer: open
        U3 under literal-exactly-three closer: closed
    while the fixture pack's own walk and the reference print `state=closed` for U1
    (transcript C). The suite should implement the fixture/CommonMark rule; the
    "exactly three" sentence would misgrade U1-class documents as RED.
F2  The claim's literal G2 grading sentence is not sufficient for the pack's six
    detail/termination limbs. Rejecting m02/m03/m04/m05 (detail-free or unrejected
    mutants) requires the `mutants=<a>/<b>` whole-line form plus the detail
    corroboration rule; rejecting m07 needs the verdict field to terminate the line;
    rejecting m08 needs `mutants=` to be mandatory. A suite that implements only
    "contains positive=pass and verdict=pass" prints `closed=1` on those six
    fixtures (transcript G). The pack's table is the discriminating contract; either
    the suite adopts it or G2's after-value must be read as weaker than the pack lets
    it appear.
F3  The G2 receipts bind no header (export-ref/ancestry/digest are 40/64 zeros by the
    pack's own README), so the pack grades the case region only. The judged-head run
    over a real export remains the only evidence for the header-binding half of G2;
    fixture agreement is not evidence for it.
F4  The G1 fixtures grade clause 3 against the fixture's own `base/`, deliberately not
    the lane's round-39 base (README: "the fixture grades the clause, not the round's
    own diff"). The reference reproduced that: git numstat over base->head matched the
    reference's diff count in all five fixtures (transcript A). On the real export the
    base ref must be 480b1a505a2dfc14a4f718d16d63808247214d0a and the head J.
