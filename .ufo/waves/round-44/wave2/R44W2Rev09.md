**Append-only:** partial only. D1 requires `13/19` to remain in the row and the matching correction to follow that row (`:190-206`), so deleting that literal is caught. It does not pin the row's other bytes: changing the row's `closed=13/19 exit=1` cell to `REWRITTEN-CELL (token 13/19 retained)` still returned `fixed`. Thus a rewrite preserving the token is not caught.
