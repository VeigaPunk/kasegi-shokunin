## Disclosures (the NOT-SHOWN mutants)

(a) H1a/H1b — the H1 counts clause and the one-live-row clause are shadowed at gate level by the
    later F29-presence/duplicate-id clauses: any tree that trips counts (rows=28/30) or duplicates
    the row also trips `dup` or `entry-shared`, so no single-defect tree isolates them. The clauses
    are belt-and-braces; the wave-1 selftest grades them on synthetic tables with a controlled id set.
(b) H2b — the per-section word floor is shadowed by the section's own table: a section that still
    carries its >= 6-row table always clears 25 words, and a section stripped below 25 words loses
    the table and trips table-rows. Graded at selftest level in wave 1.
(c) H3a — relaxing the 11-cell test to >= 9 re-parses the row but the shifted cells then fail the
    status/opened/cadence literals, so the tree stays red: the relaxation is closed by construction
    (a 9-cell row cannot satisfy the positional checks).
(d) H4b/H4c — H4 pins the manifest bytes (H4a, flip shown), so any manifest edit that would exercise
    the quote-count or distinctness floors is already red at the pin; the floors are not isolable at
    gate level. Graded at selftest level in wave 1 (the selftest rebuilds PINS for its synthetic trees).
(e) H5c — the >= 4 located floor is shadowed by `failed == 0` + the H2 table-rows >= 6 conjunct: an
    entry with a 6-row cited table always has >= 4 located spans. Graded at selftest level in wave 1.
(f) H7a/H7c — both grade the census OUTPUT shape (`rc == 0` + an `ok` tab line). The census script
    lives at the fleet root and is not mutated by this lane; a tree with census rc=0 never lacks the
    ok line, so no lane-side defect tree isolates them. Disclosed as census-dependent; the census
    itself is gated upstream (the fleet SOURCES contract).
