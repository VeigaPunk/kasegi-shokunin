  57: Below, we adapt a version of the
  58: ISO/IEC 30111:2019 Information technology—Security techniques—Vulnerability handling processes
  59: with more phases to better describe what we have seen at the CERT/CC
  60: Discovery
  61: A researcher (not necessarily an academic one) discovers a vulnerability by using one of numerous tools and processes.
  62: Reporting
  63: A researcher submits a vulnerability report to a software or product vendor, or a third-party coordinator if necessary.
  64: Validation and Prioritization
  65: The analyst validates the report to ensure accuracy before action can be taken and prioritizes reports relative to others.
  66: Remediation
  67: A remediation plan (ideally a software patch, but could also be other mechanisms) is developed and tested.
  68: Public Awareness
  69: The vulnerability and its remediation plan is disclosed to the public.
  70: Deployment
