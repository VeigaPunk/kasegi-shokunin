LEAD L2 — CloudFront signed cookies (CloudFront-Signature, CloudFront-Key-Pair-Id)

The signed-COOKIE form is a distinct shape: the names are CloudFront-Signature and
CloudFront-Key-Pair-Id and they travel in Set-Cookie headers. AWS documents it on three pages.
