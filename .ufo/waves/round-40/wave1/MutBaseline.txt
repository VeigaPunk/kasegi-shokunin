Incidental finding (pristine parser, not a mutation)
----------------------------------------------------
The xref extraction `sed -n 's/^export-ref=//p'` lifts every line STARTING with
`export-ref=`. The honest receipt's run-section transcript header starts with
`export-ref=ee5ae5e... mode=export ...`, so xref becomes two lines and
`git cat-file -t "<multiline>^{commit}"` fails: R0 shows ancestor=0,
diff-clean=0 although `git merge-base --is-ancestor ee5ae5e 8371e7a` is true
(rc=0, verified). The honest v5 receipt therefore can never satisfy G2's
ancestry limb as written. The round-40 suite should anchor its receipt parsing
to exact whole-line matches and key its transcript off a non-colliding marker.

Verdict — the baseline the r40 suite must kill
----------------------------------------------
All four mutants SURVIVE, by design of the probe: P1/P2/P3 turn an honestly-red
receipt into closed=8/8 exit=0 (R1 vs R2); M4 hides a live stray sibling behind
an unrelated scan prefix (R3 vs R4). The r39 suite's receipt grading and
scratch scan are each 2-3 find->replace edits from accepting fiction, and the
forged receipt needs no runner at all. The round-40 suite closes this class
only if: H5 grades a forged-receipt fixture whose defect survives P1/P2/P3-style
limb weakening (graded case line with mutants>=1, rejection naming the limb);
H6 grades a stray-sibling fixture against prefix-rename of its own scan (a
renamed scan must red, not silently pass); H7 binds the receipt to a
deterministic teed transcript so a hand-forged receipt fails the byte-identical
re-run. Suite bytes at 8371e7a were never modified (pristine sha256 verified
2856a210... after all runs); the lane repo was only read/cloned.
