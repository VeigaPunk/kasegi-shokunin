## Evidence discipline and frozen rubric
Evidence-to-input interpretation AND source-row mapping must both be supported.
PoC is not observed active exploitation. Internet exposure does not establish
reliable automation of all four stages. A named firewall's mere existence does
not prove effective attack interruption. Severity does not supply mission/safety
context. Supplier priority cannot be renamed deployer or CISA priority.
Missing facts are not default safe. Source component pages suggest MI support-
crippled and SI marginal assumptions on total ignorance; those are assumptions,
not observations. This dossier is an evidence-only workflow, does not silently
apply them, and does not falsely claim the source forbids defaults. Retain unknown
inputs; priority may be determinate only if every compatible table row agrees.
Otherwise abstain and request missing information. An unknown irrelevant input
need not erase a supported common outcome. Source definitions never establish
that an invented scenario exists in the world. R67 general lesson applies:
possession/mention is not necessity/effectiveness; agreement is not verification.
